# Product Context — 法采烘焙短视频脚本生成 Agent

## Product Purpose
Internal tool for the Facai (法采) bakery supplies operations team to generate short-video marketing scripts. The tool manages product data, matches video script templates, and uses AI to create tailored promotional scripts for Douyin/TikTok-style short videos selling bakery ingredients and tools.

## Users
- **Primary**: Facai operations/marketing team (2-6 people). Non-technical. Use the tool daily between kitchen and office tasks.
- **Secondary**: Occasional manager review of generated scripts.

## Physical Scene
A team member at a desk in a bright commercial bakery office. Natural light through windows, the smell of baking nearby. The screen is a 14-15 inch laptop at standard office desk distance. Ambient noise from the adjacent kitchen. Sessions are interrupted — they check data, generate a script, step away to verify product details in the kitchen, return to refine. The interface must be scannable at a glance under good ambient light.

## Register
product

## Brand
法采 (Facai) — B2B bakery ingredient and tool supplier. Practical, warm, professional. Not a luxury brand. Not tech-forward SaaS. The design should feel grounded and warm, like a well-organized commercial kitchen — not like a dashboard.

### Tone
- Direct, clear, instructional
- Warm but not cute
- Professional but not corporate
- Chinese-first, with occasional English terms where standard (AI, CSV, etc.)

### Anti-references
- SaaS dashboard aesthetic (dark sidebars, metric cards, purple gradients)
- Consumer food delivery apps (overly playful, rounded everything, food photography)
- Crypto/tech aesthetic (neon, dark mode, monospace everything)
- E-commerce marketplace (too busy, too many badges and price tags competing)

## Strategic Principles
1. **Speed over beauty** — The tool is utilitarian. Design serves task completion speed.
2. **Warm, not cold** — Avoid the sterile SaaS feel. Use warm neutrals and one committed warm accent.
3. **Chinese typography first** — Inter for UI chrome is fine, but script content areas must handle dense CJK well.
4. **Obvious affordances** — Non-technical users. Buttons look like buttons. Selected states are unambiguous.
5. **Task flow over navigation** — The generate flow (pick product → pick type → get script) is the core experience. Everything else is supporting infrastructure.
